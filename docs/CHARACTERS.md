# Characters

## Ash

**Name:** Ash  
**Age:** 15  
**Role:** Protagonist / scrap mechanic

### Visual direction
- Teenage rather than adult proportions.
- Pale/ash-gray messy hair.
- Rust-red eyes with a sleepy, unimpressed look.
- Old protective goggles.
- Layered mechanic/workshop clothing.
- Mix the visual language of a scrapyard worker and a young steampunk mechanic.
- Oversized, practical pieces rather than elegant steampunk fashion.
- Clothing should look repaired, reused and stained by actual work.

### Personality
Ash often looks tired and uninterested. He is not a silent, emotionless cool protagonist.

He can be blunt, sarcastic and lazy about things he considers pointless. He becomes noticeably more animated and talkative around machinery.

He is good at understanding objects but initially poor at understanding people.

### Character motif
“Ash” evokes ash and what remains after something has burned.

Ash repairs things others have discarded. His personal arc should gradually extend that belief from machines to people and eventually to himself.

### Dialogue direction
Keep his lines concise and dry.

Avoid making every line sarcastic. His childishness should occasionally show through, especially around discoveries, food, competition, praise of his inventions or failed experiments.

### Mechanical identity
Ash fights using self-built equipment, gadgets and modified scrap weapons. His combat identity must connect directly to crafting and tuning.

## Mina

**Role:** Repairer in Iron-scrap Town

Mina keeps a neighborhood repair stall running with parts recovered from the
old factory district. She knows the factory furnace still heats the town and
asks Ash to stop its runaway pressure without destroying the heat source.

### Visual direction
- Auburn hair tied back with a copper wire clasp.
- Patched tan work vest, tool pouch and grease-stained gloves.
- Carries a small repaired gear as a keepsake from her stall.

### Dialogue direction
Mina is direct and practical. Her concern is for the people who depend on the
furnace; she asks Ash to repair the system if possible instead of simply
breaking it.

## Azami — Demon companion and cartographer

**Age:** 16
**Role:** Ash's companion / cartographer / autonomous battle support

Azami is a demon girl found trapped beneath a shifting pile of scrap in the
Scrapyard. She is bright, trusting and fascinated by human-made machines. She
has been travelling for years with a hand-drawn map that she constantly updates
because the real world no longer matches the old one. Her straightforward
kindness gives Ash a different way to look at the people and things that others
have written off.

### Visual direction
- Small, worn charcoal horns and pointed ears; keep her visibly a demon without
  making her look threatening.
- An open, curious expression and a small, unguarded smile.
- Humble folk-inspired layers: woven indigo tunic, blue scarf, simple original
  embroidery, old beads and patched travel clothes.
- Blue-black hair, clear blue eyes, soot and repaired fabric connect her to the
  scrapyard palette. Blue is her signature color.
- The costume is original fantasy clothing, not a direct copy of a real
  culture's ceremonial dress.
- Modest teenage proportions and practical clothing.

### Personality and ability
Azami is innocent, curious and candid, with a habit of asking machines what
they need as if they can answer. She notices pressure, heat and vibration
through her horns, which lets her read the rhythm of old machines. In battle she
acts on her own: she strikes a weak seam when Ash is safe, or performs a small
emergency repair when his HP is low. She is a partner with her own judgment,
not another command menu for Ash to operate.

### Dialogue direction
Use short, bright lines and genuine curiosity. Her demon traits are part of her
identity, not a source of shame. Give her small funny observations about
machines and everyday human habits.
