# Characters

## Ash

**Name:** Ash  
**Age:** 15  
**Role:** Protagonist / scrap mechanic

### Visual direction
- Teenage rather than adult proportions.
- Pale/ash-gray messy hair.
- Rust-red eyes with a sleepy, unimpressed look.
- Old protective goggles.
- Layered mechanic/workshop clothing.
- Mix the visual language of a scrapyard worker and a young steampunk mechanic.
- Oversized, practical pieces rather than elegant steampunk fashion.
- Clothing should look repaired, reused and stained by actual work.

### Personality
Ash often looks tired and uninterested. He is not a silent, emotionless cool protagonist.

He can be blunt, sarcastic and lazy about things he considers pointless. He becomes noticeably more animated and talkative around machinery.

He is good at understanding objects but initially poor at understanding people.

### Character motif
“Ash” evokes ash and what remains after something has burned.

Ash repairs things others have discarded. His personal arc should gradually extend that belief from machines to people and eventually to himself.

### Dialogue direction
Keep his lines concise and dry.

Avoid making every line sarcastic. His childishness should occasionally show through, especially around discoveries, food, competition, praise of his inventions or failed experiments.

### Mechanical identity
Ash fights using self-built equipment, gadgets and modified scrap weapons. His combat identity must connect directly to crafting and tuning.

## Mina

**Role:** Repairer in Iron-scrap Town

Mina keeps a neighborhood repair stall running with parts recovered from the
old factory district. She knows the factory furnace still heats the town and
asks Ash to stop its runaway pressure without destroying the heat source.

### Visual direction
- Auburn hair tied back with a copper wire clasp.
- Patched tan work vest, tool pouch and grease-stained gloves.
- Carries a small repaired gear as a keepsake from her stall.

### Dialogue direction
Mina is direct and practical. Her concern is for the people who depend on the
furnace; she asks Ash to repair the system if possible instead of simply
breaking it.
