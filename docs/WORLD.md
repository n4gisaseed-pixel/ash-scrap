# World

## Direction
Industrial junk-fantasy / junkpunk with steampunk influence.

The world should feel used rather than decorative: soot, patched pipes, leaking steam, reused signage, abandoned machinery, stacked housing, workshops and improvised technology.

## Starting region

### Ash's Workshop
Ash's home and primary upgrade hub. Warm, cramped and cluttered. It should gradually change as the player brings back unusual parts and completes projects.

### Iron-scrap Town
A settlement built around salvaging and reusing industrial remains. People survive by repairing what earlier generations left behind.

### Scrapyard
The first exploration zone. It introduces salvage nodes, environmental interaction and low-level mechanical enemies.

### Abandoned Factory
Chapter 0 dungeon. Old systems are unexpectedly becoming active again.

## Technology
Steam machinery, pressure systems, mechanical drives and salvaged remnants of more advanced technology coexist.

Not every machine should be fully understood by current society.

## Narrative rule
Technology is not merely visual decoration. Machines, repair, reuse, obsolescence and ownership should matter to the story.

## Central thematic question
Who decides when a thing—or a person—has stopped being useful?
