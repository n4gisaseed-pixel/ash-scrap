# STORY BIBLE — 救われた後のセカイ

## One-line premise

Seven years after the Demon King was defeated, a junk mechanic and a demon
cartographer follow a ruined map to repair the World Cores that the old hero's
party destroyed to win the war.

## Story promise

This is the quiet journey after a familiar RPG ending. The world is broken, but
not empty: people have made homes in ruins, kept furnaces running and built
customs around scarce water and salvage. The player helps them improve their
lives while learning why the previous generation made the choices it did.

The goal is not to put the old world back exactly as it was. A repaired system
must serve people who live here now.

## Prologue — The Map After the End

**Purpose:** establish Ash, Azami, the command adventure loop and the first
evidence that the World Cores were not simply the Demon King's batteries.

1. At dawn, a radio message from Mina asks Ash to find a gear, copper wire and
   pressure cylinder for Iron-scrap Town's failing communal furnace. Ash leaves
   his leaking workshop and follows an old rail line into the scrapyard.
2. The road has collapsed. New footprints and a strip of blue cloth show that
   someone passed through recently. While searching for the repair parts, Ash
   hears a deliberate tapping rhythm beneath the wreckage.
3. The pressure cylinder's old service marking points to a jammed relief valve.
   Ash repairs it instead of forcing the machine. A full-screen rescue scene
   introduces Azami, a demon girl drawing a map as she travels. She can feel
   pulses from dormant machinery through her horns.
4. The pair follow the rail line toward the settlement. Azami compares the
   changed landscape with her outdated map; they reach town as people gather
   around a shared cooking pot. Mina explains that the furnace is both an old
   military machine and the town's heat source.
5. The player can assemble the recovered parts into PILE-01 at the workshop.
   Ash and Azami defeat the Scrap Hound, whose order still says “remove
   intruders,” and investigate the auxiliary furnace without destroying it.
6. The relay's guard wakes when the pressure changes. After the pair disable
   it, they walk the heated pipes back to town. The communal fire has returned;
   an abandoned cistern gives one drop of water and Azami's map shows a pulse
   from a much larger network.
7. The maintenance record calls the World Cores a planetary
   circulation system. It contradicts the old war story that the Cores existed
   only to feed the Demon King. The record is incomplete; the pair need to visit
   the Green Core at Whitewood to verify it.

**Closing image:** one drop in the dry cistern and a new line toward Whitewood
on Azami's map. The landscape is still ruined. Ash agrees to continue because
there is another broken thing they can understand and repair.

## Chapter 1 — Whitewood, the Green Core

**Region:** a white forest where trees and beasts have hardened into pale
crystal. The old road is gone, and the forest's guardian still follows a
centuries-old protection order.

**Key beats:**
- Azami updates the first section of her map after a safe, low-risk scouting
  trip. A local settlement asks the pair to recover a seed vault rather than
  clear-cut the crystal growth.
- The party chooses between a safe salvage route for repair material and a
  dangerous deep-forest route that can reveal an old hero record and the
  guardian's weakness.
- Ash reuses the guardian's broken branch-arm as part of a core coupling. Azami
  identifies its vibration pattern and predicts its next attack.
- The Green Core is repaired. Only one seed sprouts; characters disagree about
  whether that small change is enough.

**Reward:** a living seed sample and the first piece of a maintenance diagram
that explains the four regional Cores.

## Chapter 2 — The Dry Lake, the Water Core

**Region:** a drained lake. Its exposed bed reveals an old city, including
streets, pump houses and the remains of homes. The people above ground depend on
condensation collectors and rationed wells.

**Key beats:**
- The map is useful for the city's old layout but wrong about which structures
  remain above ground. Azami crosses out “lake” and writes “lakebed.”
- A pump-keeper and a water trader disagree about whether the first restored
  water should refill the public cistern or irrigate a small field.
- A deep route yields parts and a record of the hero's party destroying the
  Water Core. They were told the Demon King was drawing power from it; they had
  no record of its role in the watershed.
- The pump sentinel is not malicious: its safety routine has been diverting all
  water away from the cracked core.
- After repair, water runs through the street gutter. The lake remains dry.

**Reward:** a reliable cistern, a pump component for crafting and a new route
marked on Azami's map.

## Chapter 3 — The Broken Route, the Wind Core

**Region:** a highland cut off by a permanent magic storm. Wrecked airships and
rail bridges hang over deep fissures.

**Key beats:**
- Scouting is safer along the exposed railway; the storm route offers rare
  drive parts and an old recording from the hero's journey.
- Ash builds a disposable glider from airship scrap. Azami is eager to try it;
  Ash insists on testing it with a toolbox first.
- The storm guardian attacks according to a repeating wind cycle. The player
  reads the turn order and uses Azami's forecast to choose when Ash should vent
  HEAT or fire the PILE-01.
- Repairing the Wind Core opens a pass through the mountains. The storm does
  not vanish, but the route becomes crossable again.

**Reward:** an airship chart showing that the original hero's party came through
the same pass from the opposite direction.

## Chapter 4 — The Furnace City, the Fire Core

**Region:** a former industrial city. Its furnaces still keep small districts
warm, but the old control system consumes repair stock faster than residents
can replace it.

**Key beats:**
- The town wants more heat before winter. A repair guild wants to shut the
  furnace down before it destroys itself. Neither group is simply wrong.
- Ash dismantles an obsolete military drive and fits the reusable parts into
  the furnace regulator. The player decides which of two districts receives
  the first stable heat loop.
- A recording reveals that the Demon King increased the Core output to power
  weapons, hastening the collapse. The hero's party later destroyed the Fire
  Core to stop the flow.
- Ash repairs the controller and changes its load priorities rather than
  destroying the furnace.

**Reward:** a stable heat network and the authorization pattern needed to enter
the Old Demon Castle.

## Chapter 5 — The Old Demon Castle, the Crown Core

**Region:** the former final dungeon. Ash and Azami find the oldest records of
the magical circulation network, the Demon King's exploitation of it and the
hero's attempt to stop the war.

**Key beats:**
- The central Core identifies Azami as a possible successor because she is a
  demon and can synchronize with it. This is a machine classification, not a
  prophecy and not proof that she wants to become Demon King.
- Azami learns that restoring the network may make people fear her. She still
  chooses to continue, but Ash refuses to let a machine define her future.
- The central guardian is assembled from parts of the old castle's defenses.
  The party uses the map, Core patterns and repaired equipment to open it
  without destroying the archive.
- Repairing the Crown Core reconnects the five nodes. Its first result is a
  clearing in the ash clouds and rain over one rooftop garden.

**Reward:** a complete map of the five-Core network and proof that the world is
recovering slowly.

## Finale — The Next Hero

Reactivation reports reach the new kingdom. Its systems detect “Demon King
reaction confirmed” and a new hero party is sent to stop the apparent threat.
They are acting on the same incomplete story that shaped the previous war.

The final encounter is a defense and a conversation, not an execution. The
player uses Ash's non-lethal repairs and the restored Core network to survive
long enough to reveal the records. The new hero must choose whether to repeat a
successful old strategy or listen to what the world has become.

The ending leaves the world visibly damaged but changing. Azami adds one final
note to her map: the routes are no longer instructions for reaching the past;
they are directions toward people who need help now.

## Episode structure and scope

- Five World Cores: four regional nodes and the central Crown Core.
- Prologue plus five Core chapters, followed by a short defense finale.
- Each region has one safe option, one riskier route, one character/event beat
  and one boss encounter. Not every trip needs a battle.
- Current playable prototype remains the Prologue. The regional chapters are
  story plans, not implemented content yet.
